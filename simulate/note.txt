### **Strategy: Horizontal Line Retracement Play**

1. **Identify Key Levels**: The horizontal lines (e.g., 5,530.00, 5,646.75, etc.) act as support/resistance. 
   Focus on levels where price has previously reacted (e.g., 5,135.00 is the current price level with a strong drop noted).

2. **Entry Condition**:  
   - **Long Trade**: Enter **2 points above a horizontal line** (e.g., 5,137.25 if using 5,135.00) **only after**:  
     1. Price has risen **at least two lines higher** (e.g., to 5,237.25 or above).  
     2. Then **retraces back** to your entry level (5,137.25).  
   - *Rationale*: Confirms upward momentum before a pullback, increasing likelihood of continuation.

3. **Stop Loss**:  
   - Place stop **100 ticks below entry** (e.g., 5,127.25 for a long at 5,137.25).  
   - *Note*: 100 ticks in S&P E-mini = 25 points (1 tick = 0.25 points). Adjust if you meant 100 points (e.g., stop at 5,037.25).

4. **Profit Target**: 
   - Set target **100 ticks above entry** (e.g., 5,162.25 for a long at 5,137.25).  

5. **Example from Chart**:  
   - If price rises from 5,135.00 to 5,237.25 (+2 lines), then drops back to 5,137.25, go long with:  
     - Entry: 5,137.25  
     - Stop: 5,127.25 (or 5,037.25 if 100 points)  
     - Target: 5,162.25 (or 5,237.25 if 100 points).
